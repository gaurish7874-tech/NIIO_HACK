# Vital Signs Reference Guide

## Heart Rate (HR)
- **Normal resting range:** 60-100 BPM (beats per minute)
- **Tachycardia (>100 BPM):** May indicate stress, anxiety, caffeine consumption, physical exercise, dehydration, fever, or certain medications. Persistent tachycardia above 120 BPM at rest warrants medical evaluation.
- **Bradycardia (<60 BPM):** Common in well-trained athletes and during sleep. May also result from certain medications (beta-blockers). Resting HR below 40 BPM with symptoms (dizziness, fainting) requires medical attention.
- **Optimal resting HR:** 60-80 BPM for most adults. Lower is generally better for cardiovascular health.

## Blood Pressure (Estimated)
- **What it measures:** The pressure of circulating blood against the walls of blood vessels. Normal blood pressure is essential for proper blood flow.
- **Normal (<120/80 mmHg):** Indicates a healthy cardiovascular state and relaxation.
- **Elevated (120-129/<80 mmHg):** Normal range for most adults during daily activities or moderate stress.
- **High (>130/80 mmHg):** Indicates potential high stress, anxiety, or physiological strain. Persistently high blood pressure warrants professional consultation.

## Respiration Rate
- **Normal range:** 12-20 breaths per minute (RPM) for adults at rest.
- **Elevated (>20 RPM):** May indicate anxiety, hyperventilation, physical exertion, pain, or fever.
- **Low (<12 RPM):** May indicate deep relaxation, meditation, or potential sedation from medications.
- **Rapid shallow breathing:** Often associated with the stress response and can be consciously controlled through breathing exercises.

## Blink Rate
- **Normal range:** 15-20 blinks per minute in a relaxed state.
- **Elevated (>25/min):** May indicate stress, dry eyes, eye strain, bright light exposure, or anxiety.
- **Low (<10/min):** May indicate intense screen focus, cognitive load, or early drowsiness. Prolonged reduced blinking contributes to dry eye syndrome.

## Eye Closure Ratio
- **Scale:** 0.0 (fully open) to 1.0 (fully closed)
- **Normal alert state:** 0.0-0.2
- **Drowsiness warning:** >0.4 indicates prolonged or frequent eye closures
- **Significant drowsiness:** >0.6 suggests microsleep episodes may be occurring

## Gaze Stability
- **Scale:** 0.0 (highly erratic) to 1.0 (perfectly stable)
- **Stable (>0.7):** Indicates focused attention and calm state
- **Moderate instability (0.3-0.7):** May indicate mild distraction or moderate anxiety
- **Highly unstable (<0.3):** Suggests significant distraction, anxiety, or restlessness

## Head Pose Indicators
- **Excessive downward pitch (>15°):** May indicate drowsiness or falling asleep
- **Frequent yaw changes:** Suggests restlessness or environmental distraction
- **Head roll (>10°):** May indicate discomfort, fatigue, or drowsiness
