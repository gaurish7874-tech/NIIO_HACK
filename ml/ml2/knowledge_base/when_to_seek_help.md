# When to Seek Professional Help

## Important Disclaimer
This contactless wellness monitoring system provides informational insights about your physiological and behavioral state. It is NOT a medical device, does NOT provide medical diagnoses, and should NOT be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.

## Seek Immediate Medical Attention If:

### Cardiovascular Warning Signs
- Resting heart rate consistently above **120 BPM** without obvious cause (exercise, caffeine)
- Resting heart rate below **40 BPM** with symptoms (dizziness, lightheadedness, fainting)
- Chest pain, tightness, or pressure combined with elevated heart rate
- Heart palpitations (feeling your heart racing, skipping beats) lasting more than a few minutes
- Sudden onset of rapid heartbeat with shortness of breath

### Respiratory Warning Signs
- Persistent breathing rate above **25 breaths per minute** at rest
- Shortness of breath that worsens when lying down
- Difficulty catching your breath during normal activities
- Hyperventilation that does not respond to breathing exercises within 10 minutes

## Consult a Healthcare Professional If:

### Persistent Stress Indicators
- Stress signals (elevated HR, low HRV) remain consistently elevated over multiple days despite self-help techniques
- You experience persistent anxiety that interferes with daily functioning
- Physical stress symptoms: frequent headaches, muscle tension, digestive issues, or sleep problems
- HRV consistently below **15 ms** combined with fatigue or mood changes

### Sleep and Fatigue Concerns
- Persistent daytime drowsiness despite adequate sleep (7-9 hours)
- Frequent involuntary microsleep episodes (brief moments of falling asleep)
- Difficulty staying awake during routine activities
- Suspected sleep disorder: loud snoring, gasping during sleep (reported by others), or waking unrefreshed

### Mental Health
- Persistent feelings of hopelessness, worthlessness, or inability to enjoy activities
- Thoughts of self-harm or suicide — contact emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately
- Severe anxiety or panic attacks that do not respond to self-help techniques
- Significant changes in behavior, appetite, or daily functioning lasting more than two weeks

## Emergency Resources
- **Emergency Services:** Call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 112 in the EU, 999 in the UK, 112 in India)
- **Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741 (US), 85258 (UK)
- **SAMHSA Helpline:** 1-800-662-4357 (US, free, confidential, 24/7)
- **iCall (India):** 9152987821
- **Vandrevala Foundation (India):** 1860-2662-345 (24/7)

## What This System Can and Cannot Do

### What it CAN do:
- Track trends in your physiological and behavioral signals over time
- Provide immediate self-help suggestions for common conditions (stress, fatigue)
- Alert you when signals suggest you should take a break or seek attention
- Help you understand your body's stress and relaxation patterns

### What it CANNOT do:
- Diagnose any medical condition
- Replace a doctor, therapist, or other healthcare professional
- Detect medical emergencies with clinical accuracy
- Measure blood pressure, blood oxygen, or other clinical vital signs
